# 10xCards — Shape Notes

## Vision & Problem Statement

Manual creation of high-quality educational flashcards is time-consuming and exhausting. Learners who recognize the value of spaced repetition often abandon it because creation friction dominates their study time — authoring front/back cards takes longer than reviewing them.

10xCards leverages LLMs to instantly distill raw text (articles, documentation, course material) into effective atomic Q&A flashcards. This shifts the user's role from manual card author to fast card reviewer and editor, eliminating creation overhead while keeping spaced-repetition study active and efficient.

## User & Persona

### Primary Persona: Upskilling Professional

- **Profile**: Working professional (e.g. software engineer, specialist) upskilling via articles, technical docs, and courses.
- **The Moment**: Reading dense material, identifying key concepts to retain, but dreading manual card creation.
- **Goal**: Quickly paste raw text, inspect AI-generated candidate flashcards, edit/accept them with low effort, and review them using spaced repetition.

## Access Control

- **Authentication**: Standard user account creation via Email/Password or Passwordless Magic Link to ensure cards persist across devices and browser sessions.
- **Role Model**: Flat user model. Every user has full ownership of their private decks and cards. No multi-tenant admin or organization hierarchy in MVP.
- **Data Boundaries**: Users can only access and modify their own generated and manual flashcards.

## Success Criteria

### Primary
- **75% AI Card Acceptance Rate**: At least 75% of candidate flashcards generated by AI are accepted (or edited and saved) by the user.
- **75% AI Creation Preference**: At least 75% of total flashcards added to user decks are created via AI generation rather than manual entry.

### Secondary
- **Regular Review Habit**: Users complete at least one spaced-repetition study session per week.

### Guardrails
- **Generation Latency Floor**: AI card generation returns candidate flashcards in under 10 seconds for text inputs up to 2,000 words.
- **Zero Data Loss**: All accepted and edited flashcards are persisted immediately and accurately across browser refreshes and re-logins.

## User Stories

### US-01: AI Flashcard Generation from Text
- **Given** a logged-in learner with a block of text (article/docs)
- **When** they paste the text into the generation field and click "Generate Cards"
- **Then** they see a queue of candidate flashcards containing atomic Front (question) and Back (answer) fields within 10 seconds.

#### Acceptance Criteria
- Input accepts up to 2,000 words of plain text.
- AI extracts key concepts and formats them into distinct front/back cards.
- If text is too short (< 20 words), an inline guidance prompt is shown.

### US-02: Candidate Review and Selection
- **Given** a learner viewing a queue of AI-generated candidate cards
- **When** they modify card text, uncheck unwanted cards, and click "Save Selected Cards"
- **Then** only the approved cards are added to their active deck with default spaced repetition initial states.

#### Acceptance Criteria
- Learner can inline-edit the Front and Back text of any candidate card.
- Learner can dismiss/reject individual cards with one click.
- Bulk "Save Selected" saves all active candidate cards to the user's account.

### US-03: Spaced Repetition Study Session
- **Given** a learner with flashcards due for review today
- **When** they launch a study session, view a front prompt, flip to reveal the answer, and select a recall rating (Again, Hard, Good, Easy)
- **Then** the card's interval and next due date are updated instantly via the spaced repetition algorithm, and the next card is presented.

#### Acceptance Criteria
- Card answer remains hidden until the learner taps "Reveal Answer".
- Rating options (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) display predicted next review intervals (e.g. 1d, 3d, 6d).
- Completing all due cards shows a celebration/completion screen with summary stats.

## Functional Requirements

### Authentication & User Management
- FR-001: Learner can create an account using email and password or magic link. Priority: must-have
- FR-002: Learner can sign in and sign out securely across browser sessions. Priority: must-have

### AI Flashcard Generation
- FR-003: Learner can paste raw text (up to 2,000 words) into an input area to request flashcard generation. Priority: must-have
- FR-004: Learner can view AI-generated candidate flashcards (front/question and back/answer) in a review queue. Priority: must-have
- FR-005: Learner can accept, edit, or reject individual candidate flashcards before saving them. Priority: must-have

### Flashcard Management
- FR-006: Learner can manually create a flashcard with front (question) and back (answer) fields. Priority: must-have
- FR-007: Learner can view, edit, and delete any saved flashcards in their collection. Priority: must-have

### Spaced Repetition & Study
- FR-008: Learner can start a study session presenting due flashcards according to an integrated spaced repetition algorithm. Priority: must-have
- FR-009: Learner can rate their recall effort (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) for each card during a study session. Priority: must-have
- FR-010: Learner can see next scheduled review dates updated automatically upon rating recall effort. Priority: must-have

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Generation Latency**: AI flashcard candidate generation from source text up to 2,000 words completes within 10 seconds.
- **UI Responsiveness**: Key interactions (card flipping, recall rating, candidate editing) respond within 100 ms.
- **Data Privacy & Isolation**: Source text and generated decks are strictly private to the user account and not retained by third-party LLM providers for model training.
- **Persistence Guarantee**: Review progress and schedule updates are saved immediately to prevent loss if the session is interrupted.
- **Cross-Browser Compatibility**: Fully functional on modern desktop and mobile browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

## Business Logic

Flashcards generated from source text must be atomic single-concept Q&A pairs, where recall ratings (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) during study dynamically calculate each card's next review interval and memory stability using a standardized spaced repetition algorithm.

### Domain Rules & Flow
1. **Atomic Extraction**: Text input is transformed into standalone front (question) and back (answer) prompts focusing on single facts or core concepts. Compound or ambiguous questions are split or discarded.
2. **Interval Calculation**: Rating recall effort during study modifies card stability and interval:
   - **Again**: Resets card review interval to immediate/short term.
   - **Hard / Good / Easy**: Expands the review interval progressively based on previous stability and difficulty factor.
3. **Deck State Integrity**: Cards retain review history logs to calculate scheduling accurately across devices.

## Non-Goals

- **No Custom Spaced Repetition Algorithm**: Integrate an established open-source algorithm (e.g. FSRS or SuperMemo-2 library) instead of building custom scheduling math.
- **No File Import (PDF, DOCX, EPUB)**: MVP relies strictly on copy-pasted raw text input.
- **No Deck Sharing or Public Decks**: Decks are strictly private to the user account in MVP.
- **No Native Mobile Apps**: MVP is a responsive web application only.
- **No Third-Party Platform Integrations**: No Anki import/export or Notion sync in MVP.
